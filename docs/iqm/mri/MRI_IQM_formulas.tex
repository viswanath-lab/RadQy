\section{Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)}
\begin{equation}
\text{SNR} = \frac{\mu_{\text{signal}}}{\sigma_{\text{noise}}}
\end{equation}


where $\mu_{\text{signal}}$ is the mean intensity in the signal region and $\sigma_{\text{noise}}$ is the standard deviation in a background noise region.
